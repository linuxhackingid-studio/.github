## About

**PT Linuxhackingid Cyber Security** is an Indonesian cybersecurity company founded in **2019**, serving organizations across **Indonesia and ASEAN**. Linuxhackingid brings together a community, an academy, offensive security services, and a security platform in one ecosystem, covering everything from training the next generation of practitioners to testing production systems at banks, telcos, government agencies, and digital platforms.

This GitHub organization, **Linuxhackingid Studio**, hosts our open-source security tools, utilities, and engineering projects.

## What We Do

| Business Unit | Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Offensive Security Services** | Penetration testing, red team emulation, and internal testing for banking, telecom, government, and digital platforms |
| **Vulnerability Research** | 0-day research, reverse engineering, fuzzing, exploit development, and source and binary secure code review |
| **Academy** | Hands-on online courses, bootcamps, and corporate training programs, with publicly verifiable certificates |
| **Platform** | **Synapse CTEM** (continuous threat exposure management, early access), free Public Tools, and Temp Mail |

### Engagement Standards

- **Methodology:** testing aligned with **PTES**, **OWASP ASVS**, and **MITRE ATT&CK**
- **Accountability:** named operators and no subcontracting
- **Assurance:** retest within **14 days** and an attestation letter with every engagement
- **Governance:** aligned with **ISO 27001**, mapped to **PCI-DSS** and **OJK (POJK 11/POJK.03/2022)**, with attention to **UU PDP No. 27/2022**. Every engagement runs under an NDA, written rules of engagement, and controlled evidence handling

## Responsible Disclosure

If you find a security vulnerability in any Linuxhackingid software, tool, or infrastructure, **do not open a public issue**. Report it privately to **[support@linuxhackingid.com](mailto:support@linuxhackingid.com)** with enough detail for us to reproduce it. PGP is available on request. We'll acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you if you'd like.
